Classic Tombala and 75-ball Bingo with cute rivals. Free play, no real money.

Tombala and Bingo, the cheerful number games, now in your pocket. Mark your numbers, race the friendly rivals and call it before they do!

Features

The classic Turkish game: cards with 3 rows and 15 numbers

Win the 1st line, the 2nd line and the final Tombala, in order

Play with 1 to 3 cards at a time

5x5 cards with the B-I-N-G-O columns and a free centre star

Four goal patterns: line, 4 corners, X and full card

Play with 1 to 4 cards at a time

Three rivals play every round and call their wins at different speeds

A small counter shows how close each rival is to calling

Tap a drawn number on your card to mark it, then press the big button first

Auto-mark and auto-call options for a relaxed game, or turn them off for a real challenge

Drawn but unmarked numbers softly pulse so you never miss one

Choose the ball speed and how fast the rivals react

An optional voice caller reads each number aloud (uses your device's text-to-speech voice)

Plays offline, no account needed

Points, stats and 10 badges to collect

Sign in with Google Play Games for achievements, leaderboards and cloud save

Just for fun: there is no real money, no betting and no real prizes. This is not a gambling app

Ads appear between some rounds; one optional purchase removes them for good

Available in English, Türkçe, Español and Português
