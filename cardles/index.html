Features

Four classics

Klondike (draw 1 or 3), Spider (1, 2 or 4 suits), FreeCell and TriPeaks. Every game has a short how-to-play guide.

Cards with faces

Cards that blink, follow your finger and cheer when you win. Leave them alone for a while and they fall asleep.

Daily challenge

Everyone gets the same deal each day and the game changes daily. Keep your streak and get an optional evening reminder.

Smart hints

Hints follow a real solution. Undo is unlimited and always free, and the game tells you when no moves are left.

Achievements and leaderboards

30 achievements and 10 leaderboards with Google Play Games: fastest wins, fewest moves, longest streaks.

Themes, offline, no account

Four table themes that unlock as you win and an optional 4-colour deck. Plays offline, no account needed. Available in English, Turkish, Spanish and Portuguese.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. An ad is shown now and then between games, and you can optionally watch a video for extra hints. Undo is always free.

Do I need internet?

No, the games work offline. Ads, leaderboards and achievements need a connection.

Is every deal solvable?

In FreeCell and TriPeaks, yes. In Klondike and Spider the game tells you when no moves are left and you can replay the same deal.

Do I need Google Play Games?

No, it is optional. Without signing in the game works the same; you just won't appear on leaderboards or earn achievements.
