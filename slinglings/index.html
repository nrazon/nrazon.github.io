Slinglings

Sling cute couriers through space, bend shots around planets, deliver the mail!

Sling adorable couriers across space and deliver the mail! Meet the Slinglings: round, fluffy mail couriers who love to be flung through the cosmos. Pull back the sling, aim along the dotted line and let go. Planets pull your courier with gravity, so every shot bends and curves. Reach the glowing mailbox to deliver the letter, and grab the stamps on the way for up to 3 stars.

Features

12 colorful routes with 20 levels each, plus bonus postcards and tough Special Deliveries

11 couriers with their own tricks: speedy Zip, floaty Puff, splitting Duo, dropping Plop, flipping Clink, boomerang Rang, teleporting Blink, heavy Tubby, ghostly Wisp and sticky Gum

New twists on every route: black holes, bouncy bumpers, crates, crystals, mail slots, pipes, magnets, keys and locked doors, ink clouds and moving planets

Every level is checked by a solver, so it can always be won

Stuck? A hint shows the exact shot and where to pull

A fresh Daily Letter every day, with a streak to keep

Collect stamps for 3 stars, find hidden lost letters and hit side goals: Fast, Close call and Clean

Dress your courier with 12 accessories from the Locker and collect 33 badges

No timer, no lives, no pressure. Plays offline, no account needed

Choose how you aim: Classic, Slow pull or Lock & fire

Cheerful music that grows as you collect stamps, bouncy animations, sounds and vibration (each can be turned off)

Sign in with Google Play Games for achievements, leaderboards and cloud save

Ads are rare: none in the first levels, and extra-hint videos are always optional. One small purchase removes ads for good

Available in English, Türkçe, Español and Português
