Sheep Pens: How to Solve Rectangle Division Puzzles

By Nesim Razon, independent mobile developer

Sheep Pens is a rectangle puzzle. Some squares hold a sheep with a number, and you split the whole grid into rectangular pens so that every pen holds exactly one sheep and covers as many squares as its number. It is calm, visual and fully solvable by logic.

The rules

  • Drag your finger from one square to another to draw a rectangle. Tap a pen to erase it.
  • Every pen must contain exactly one sheep, and its area (the number of squares it covers) must equal the number on that sheep.
  • Pens cannot overlap and must be rectangles. When every square belongs to a correct pen, the puzzle is solved.
  • Every puzzle has exactly one solution, so you never need to guess.

Where to start

  • A sheep with a 1 is its own pen. Draw it first.
  • Look at the biggest numbers. A 6, for example, can only be a 1x6, 2x3, 3x2 or 6x1 rectangle, and walls and other sheep rule most of those out.
  • Squares that only one sheep can reach are forced: if just one pen can possibly cover a square, it belongs to that pen.
  • Two sheep close together split the space between them. Count how many squares each one needs and see where the border has to fall.

Keep every square in play

The trick that solves harder grids is to ask, for each empty square, which sheep could still reach it. A pen cannot swallow another sheep and cannot leave the grid, and its size is fixed by the number. When only one option is left for a square, the pen is decided. When none is left, an earlier pen was wrong, so use Undo.

Frequently asked questions

Can a pen have two sheep?

No. Every pen holds exactly one sheep, and its size must match the number on it.

Do I have to guess?

No. Every puzzle has a single solution that can be found by logic alone.

How do I remove a pen I drew?

Tap the pen. You can also use Undo or Reset.

Which grid sizes are there?

From 5x5 to 9x9, in Easy, Medium and Hard.
