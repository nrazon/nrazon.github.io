Snowlings Guide: New Year Countdown and Winter Calendar

By Nesim Razon, independent mobile developer

Snowlings puts a New Year countdown and a 31-door winter calendar on the first screen. It is simple once you know what each part does and where it gets its time from. This guide explains how the countdown is calculated, when each door opens, how reminders and the widget behave, and what the extra tools are for.

Where to find the countdown

Open Snowlings and you land on the Calendar tab. At the top there is a panel titled Until the New Year with four numbers: days, hours, minutes and seconds. It updates every second, so you can leave the phone on the table and watch it tick. Below it sits the tree, and under the tree the grid of doors.

The countdown is not limited to December. In July it simply counts down to the next January 1, so the panel never shows an empty or broken state.

How the countdown is calculated

The target is midnight at the start of January 1 in your own time zone, and the current time comes from your phone's clock. That has two practical consequences. First, you never type in a city or a time zone: the app reads the zone your phone is using. Second, if your phone clock is wrong, the countdown will be wrong too, so keep automatic date and time switched on in the system settings.

Daylight saving time needs no attention from you. The app works with real time-zone rules, so the remaining time is the number of real hours between now and your local midnight, even if your country moves its clocks between today and the new year. The app reads your time zone when it starts. If you fly across zones with the app still open, close it fully and reopen it to be safe.

The Midnight screen

For a bigger view, open the Tools tab and choose Midnight. It shows the days and a HH:MM:SS clock, and in the last ten seconds the number grows into one large figure. When the clock reaches zero the screen switches to Happy New Year! with fireworks and fireworks sound. The small fireworks button below the clock lets you preview the show without waiting.

Under the clock, two lists show who is already in the New Year and which cities come next. They cover 22 cities, from Kiritimati and Auckland through Tokyo, Istanbul and London to New York and Honolulu, each with its December time offset. It is a fun thing to follow on the evening of December 31. On January 1 and 2 the screen keeps showing the celebration; after that it counts down to the following year.

How the 31 doors work

  • From December 1, one door opens per day, at midnight local time. Door 5 opens on December 5, and so on.
  • Today's door glows gold. Doors that are still ahead show a lock; tap one and the app tells you which December date it opens.
  • Each day has its own puzzle. The games rotate through Ice Slide, Gift Sort, Winter Mahjong, Snow Memory, Sled Run and Paint the Picture. Days 1 to 3 are the easiest, the hardest puzzles come between days 22 and 30.
  • December 24 and 25 are special large Paint the Picture puzzles, and door 31 is the finale that lights the star on top of the tree.
  • A solved door turns into an ornament with up to three stars, and the same ornament appears on your tree.

After you solve today's door, the calendar shows how long until tomorrow's door opens. When all 31 doors are solved the tree is complete, and a button lets you share a picture of it.

November, missed doors and winter mode

In November the calendar says the doors open on December 1. You can already try the practice door, and the games and tools are open all month.

If you skip a day in December, that door is marked as missed, but it is never lost. You can open it by watching a short ad or by spending 3 snowflakes. Snowflakes are earned by winning free play levels, up to 5 per day. If you install the app during December, the doors before your install day are open without any ad, so a late start is not punished.

In January and February the app switches to winter mode: every door is open, your tree stays as the keepsake of the year and you can replay any door. From March to October the doors can still be replayed.

Reminders and the home screen widget

To get a nudge when a door opens, go to Settings (the gear button at the top of the Calendar and Tools tabs) and switch on Notifications. On recent Android versions the phone will ask for permission first. You can then pick the time with the minus and plus buttons: the default is 09:00 and the range is 06:00 to 22:00.

With reminders on, you get a notification for every door from December 1 to 31, one more at 23:00 on December 31 saying one hour is left, and a Happy New Year! message at midnight. If you have saved a wish capsule, it has its own notification at 10:00 on December 31. Because the app uses flexible alarms, a reminder can arrive a few minutes after the chosen time. The reminders are set again after a restart of the phone.

The Android widget is a compact 2x2 tile for the home screen. It shows the number of days until the New Year and, in December, the text Door N is open. It counts whole calendar days, so on December 31 it says 1 while the app shows 0 days and a number of hours. On January 1 to 3 it shows a fireworks symbol and Happy New Year!.

The other tools and your progress

  • Greeting card: choose one of 24 designs, pick a message group (family, friends, work or love), edit the text, add a name or a photo from your gallery, and share the finished card in any app.
  • Wish capsule: write up to three wishes and seal them. The capsule can be opened on the next December 31, and the text stays on your device.
  • Snow globe: shake the phone and it snows. There are four scenes to choose from.

On Android, Snowlings can back up your progress with Play Games saved games: door stars, opened doors, level stars and the total solved count. When two devices disagree, the higher number wins, so progress never goes backwards. Your wish text, settings, snowflakes, hints and photos stay on the device. Settings also lets you pick the language (English, Turkish, Spanish or Portuguese) and reset progress, so use that last button with care.

Frequently asked questions

Does the countdown use my time zone?

Yes. It counts down to midnight at the start of January 1 in the time zone your phone is set to, using your phone's clock.

Do I need to adjust anything for daylight saving time?

No. The app uses real time-zone rules, so a clock change before the new year is already accounted for.

What if I miss a door?

It is never lost. In December you can open it by watching a short ad or spending 3 snowflakes, and in January and February every door is open.

Why did a reminder arrive a few minutes late?

The app uses flexible Android alarms that do not need the exact-alarm permission, so the system may deliver a reminder a little after the chosen time.

Does the widget show the same number as the app?

Almost. The widget counts whole calendar days, while the app counts the real time left down to the second, so the two can differ by one on the last day.
