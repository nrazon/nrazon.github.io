Logiclings

Calm daily logic puzzles: Crown, Twin and Path. New puzzles every day!

Logiclings is a cozy collection of logic puzzles with three kinds of brain teasers, a fresh set every day, and cute little faces cheering you on. Every day brings the same three puzzles for everyone: a Crown, a Twin and a Path. Solve them, keep your streak going and compare your times with other players on the leaderboards.

Features

Crown: place exactly one crown in every row, column and colored region, and never let two crowns touch (diagonals count too).

Twin: fill the grid with blue and orange faces. Every row and column needs the same number of each, no three alike in a row, and the = and × signs tell you which neighbors match.

Path: start at number 1, pass the numbers in order and fill every single cell with one unbroken path.

No timer pressure, no lives, no pressure at all. Take your time!

Learn by playing: a friendly tutorial for each puzzle type, and hints that explain the logic step by step

Easy to hard, from 4x4 up to 10x10 grids, so there is always a puzzle that fits your mood

Every puzzle has exactly one solution

Soft sounds, bouncy animations and vibration (you can turn them off)

Works offline, no account needed

Daily streak, stars and personal best times

Achievements and leaderboards with Google Play Games

Cloud save with Google Play Games, so you can continue on a new phone

No ads in your first puzzles, and only an occasional ad between puzzles, never while you solve
