Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Brassline ("the game") is a card room app with blackjack, European roulette and Texas Hold'em played with free play chips. It is for adults (18+) and for entertainment only: there is no real money, there are no prizes and chips cannot be bought or cashed out. It is made by Nesim Razon, an individual developer ("I", "me"). The game has no accounts, I do not sell any data and I do not operate servers for the game.

Your chips and progress

Your chip balance, statistics, settings, your age confirmation and a blackjack hand in progress are stored on your phone. If you sign in to Google Play Games, your chip balance and statistics are also saved to Google's cloud with Play Games Saved Games, tied to your Google Play Games profile, so you can continue on another phone; I cannot see it. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include your data in your device backup to your Google account. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes the data on your phone.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner in the lobby only, an occasional ad when you go from a table back to the lobby (never while you are playing at a table) and optional ads you choose to watch for a free chip refill. Gambling ads are blocked. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent first; you can change your choice in the game under Settings → Privacy choices.

Google Play Games

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, the game saves your chip balance and statistics there so cloud save works. The game has no leaderboards or achievements. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. If you do not sign in, nothing is sent.

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The game sends anonymous events to Google Analytics for Firebase, such as "a table was opened" or "a round ended" with the game type, bet size and result in play chips. They are linked to a random installation ID, not to you, and the advertising ID is not collected for analytics.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes, it sends a technical crash report (error details, device model, Android and app version) linked to a random installation ID. It contains no personal information.

Adults only

The game is intended for adults aged 18 and over and asks you to confirm your age when you first open it. It is not directed to children and I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Your rights and changes

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, Brazil's LGPD or Turkey's KVKK), you may have rights over your personal data. Because I do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data should be made to Google. If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
