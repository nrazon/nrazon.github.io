Features

Solitaire: clear the stacked layouts by matching free tiles. 53 layouts, three difficulties, and every deal is guaranteed solvable.

Connect: join two identical tiles with a path of at most two turns. 150 levels, and in many of them the tiles slide after every match.

Tile Match: tap tiles into your 7-slot rack and collect three of a kind before it fills up. 300 levels from relaxed to brain-bending.

Switch between classic Mahjong tiles and 36 cute characters: aliens, blocks, animals, weather and more

Unlock new tables and tile materials with your stars: wood, midnight blue, pastel, jade...

The daily trio: one Solitaire, one Connect and one Tile Match board, the same for everyone. Keep your streak going!

Time Attack: five minutes on the clock, every match adds time. How far can you get?

Relaxed mode has no timer at all; timed mode is there when you want a challenge

Hints, shuffle and undo when you get stuck, and a friendly tutorial for every mode

Soft sounds, calm music and gentle animations, all optional

High contrast mode, reduced motion and more

Works offline, no account needed, your game is saved when you leave

Stars, personal best times, statistics and a daily streak

Achievements, leaderboards and cloud save with Google Play Games

No ads in your first puzzles, only an occasional ad between puzzles, never while you play

Prefer no ads? One optional purchase removes them for good
